Privacy Policy

Version 2.0 · 2026-11-04

1. Data controller

Yaşar Güven Ateş (natural person; YGATES Stratejik Yönetim ve Danışmanlık) · Akçaalan Mah. No: 3, Serik / Antalya, Türkiye · yasarguvenates@gmail.com ("we"). This notice is provided under the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK), Art. 10, and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), Art. 13.

2. The product does not send your content to us

2.1. Audit Vault is an Obsidian folder that runs on your computer. Records you enter into the vault are not sent to us, and we have no access to your vault. The product contains no tracking, telemetry or analytics.

2.2. The only network requests the product makes are:

Request What is sent Where When
License activation and validation License key, instance ID, a randomly generated label (starts with DV-; contains no vault name or personal information) Lemon Squeezy license server (USA) At first setup and at most every 30 days
Version check Nothing is sent; a public version file is read This website (GitHub Pages, USA) At most once a week
Confirmation reminder e-mail (optional) Action ID, action title, days pending, due-date status, note name Your e-mail server and the recipients you choose When actions are awaiting confirmation

2.3. With the first two requests your device's IP address, as part of how the internet works, reaches the server concerned and may appear in that provider's logs. We do not receive these IP addresses.

2.4. E-mail reminders: if you enable this feature, e-mails are sent through your own SMTP server and do not reach us. You or your organization are the controller for this processing. We recommend not putting personal or confidential information in action titles.

2.5. Stored on your device: the license validation status, the reminder recipient list, the sending log and (if you choose) the SMTP password are kept only in the Obsidian local store of your own device. The SMTP password is stored unencrypted; device security is your responsibility. This data is not sent to us.

3. This website

This website uses no cookies, analytics or advertising tools, loads no external fonts or scripts, and has no data-collection forms. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process visitors' IP addresses in server logs for security purposes (GitHub Privacy Statement). Your language preference is stored only in your own browser.

4. Data processed when you buy and ask for support

4.1. Online payments are taken by Lemon Squeezy (Link, LLC) as merchant of record. Your card details never reach us; they are processed by Lemon Squeezy and its payment providers under their own privacy policies.

4.2. Lemon Squeezy shares with us what we need to fulfil your order: name, e-mail address, country, (if provided) company name and tax ID; order number, license or update pack purchased, amount, date and license key.

4.3. Pre-payment consent record: a record of the consent you give before payment (time of consent, version of the consent text, order number and license ID) is kept to prove our statutory obligations concerning the right of withdrawal. The record does not contain your name or e-mail address.

4.4. If you e-mail us, we process your message and contact details. For corporate quotes we process the company name, address and tax details needed for invoicing.

4.5. We do not send marketing messages. We e-mail you only about your order, license, updates and support requests. If we ever wish to send promotional or announcement e-mails, we will first obtain your separate express consent (Law No. 6563).
